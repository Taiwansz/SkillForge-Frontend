## Brand thesis

ThLoop proves that technical rigor and visual elegance are not opposites. Every project is functional before it is beautiful, and beautiful because it is functional. The brand occupies the space where advanced engineering meets radical restraint.

Core attributes:

- precise, not sterile;
- luxurious, not ornamental;
- technical, not performatively futuristic;
- bold, not loud;
- experimental, not chaotic;
- calm, tested, and production-minded.

## Identity architecture

### Primary: Continuous Core

The wordmark is one engineered process. The path enters at `T`, travels through `h` and `L`, iterates through both `o` chambers, then returns beneath the word and resolves at `p`. The diagonal gold notch identifies the exact handoff into iteration.

Do not typeset the name as a substitute for the wordmark in brand-signature contexts. Plain text `ThLoop` is allowed in prose, breadcrumbs, metadata, and accessibility labels.

### Compact: Twin Chamber

Twin Chamber extracts the two iteration chambers and their return path. It communicates “iterate twice, then return.” Use it only when a compact square-ish mark is required. Keep both internal counters fully open at small sizes.

### Graphic language: Chicane Cut

Chicane Cut magnifies the handoff event between a lower-left incoming path and an upper-right receiving path. The gold diagonal remains the separating registration cut. It can leave the canvas, crop imagery, interrupt rules, mask transitions, or form patterns.

## Color system

| Token | Hex | Role | Typical share |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Loop Black | `#0A0A0A` | Absolute base, primary dark surface | 60–80% |
| Technical Paper | `#F0EDE6` | Primary text on dark, light surface | 15–35% |
| Chicane Gold | `#C8B560` | Signature cut, critical selection, one focal metric | 1–3% |
| Chassis | `#2A2A2A` | Raised dark layer, secondary surface | 5–20% |
| Smoked Mirror | `#5C5C5C` | Metadata, borders, secondary type | 5–15% |

Use `#F0EDE6`, not pure white, for primary brand surfaces. Use Chicane Gold to signal importance, never to make the interface “feel premium.” Functional status colors may be added only when the product requires them; keep them muted, label them textually, and never replace the brand accent.

## Typography

The logo is custom artwork and does not define the UI font.

- Display/UI: prefer `Space Grotesk`, then `Arial`, then a geometric sans-serif system fallback.
- Technical/support: prefer `IBM Plex Mono`, then `JetBrains Mono`, then `ui-monospace`.
- Use tabular numerals for metrics, timestamps, IDs, coordinates, and specifications.
- Keep display tracking slightly tight at large sizes; use open tracking only for small uppercase labels.
- Recommended body measure: 45–72 characters.
- Avoid ultra-thin UI text. The wordmark may be bold and engineered; interface text must remain legible.

## Composition

- Use a 4px base with an 8px primary spacing rhythm.
- Favor asymmetric balance and useful negative space.
- Build hierarchy through scale, position, contrast, and surface—not repeated containers.
- Use one dominant focal object per viewport.
- Treat thin rules and Chicane interruptions as navigational structure.
- Default corner radii: 0, 4, or 8px. Use 12px only for device-like controls; avoid pill-shaped containers except tags and compact statuses.
- Prefer black-on-black depth via material contrast before borders or shadows.

## Material and image direction

Preferred materials: smoked titanium, matte powder coat, anodized aluminum, carbon fiber, warm uncoated paper, smoked acrylic, precise embroidery.

Preferred lighting: one directional source, controlled shadows, grazing light that reveals surface. Preferred photography: sparse, tactile, architectural, no decorative props.

Avoid literal cars, circuit boards, server racks, hackers, random code, glowing grids, blue neon, abstract infinity shapes, or people pointing at screens.

## Absolute prohibitions

- No gradient fills.
- No glow or bloom.
- No generic purple/blue AI palette.
- No gold metallic flood fills.
- No separate icon invented from random initials.
- No rejected old compact emblem or Core Junction proposal.
- No Chicane Cut used as the compact logo.
- No logo distortion, recoloring outside approved variants, effects, outlines, or enclosing badges.
