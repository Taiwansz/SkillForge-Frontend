# ThLoop voice and content

## Voice

Write directly, densely, and without startup ornament. ThLoop communicates with the calm confidence of something already tested in production.

### Principles

- State what works, what failed, what changed, and what is measurable.
- Prefer exact nouns and active verbs.
- Use technical terms only when they improve precision.
- Keep claims falsifiable.
- Replace excitement with evidence.
- Use short declarative headlines and slightly denser technical support copy.

### Good examples

- `From drawing to verified system.`
- `12 conflicts isolated. 9 resolved automatically.`
- `Trace the decision. Reproduce the result.`
- `Ready for review.`
- `Validation failed at node P-104. Inspect dependency.`

### Avoid

- `Revolutionize your workflow.`
- `Supercharge your productivity.`
- `The future of AI is here.`
- `Seamless, powerful, intuitive.`
- Exclamation marks, hype, vague superlatives, decorative jargon, and emoji in official brand copy.

## UI writing

- Buttons name the outcome: `Run validation`, `Export evidence`, `Open dependency map`.
- Errors explain cause and recovery: `The source drawing is unreadable. Upload a higher-resolution file or continue with manual review.`
- Empty states explain why and provide one next action.
- Loading states name the operation: `Tracing connections…`, not `Please wait…`.
- Use sentence case for UI labels; reserve uppercase monospaced text for small system metadata.

## Headline patterns

- Outcome: `Verified before presentation.`
- Tension: `Elegance without ambiguity.`
- System statement: `One path. Two iterations. A resolved output.`
- Evidence: `Every result keeps its trace.`
