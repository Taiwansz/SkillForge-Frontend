# Brand foundations

## Core proposition

TokLang is a compact intermediate language for reducing repeated natural-language tokens while preserving meaning and reversibility. The brand should make **compression visible** without looking like a compression utility or a generic AI startup.

## Personality

| Trait | Express it through | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Precise | strict spacing, real metrics, disciplined hierarchy | sterile grids |
| Fast | directional flow, short transitions, compact labels | speed lines and lightning bolts |
| Inventive | asymmetric curves, cropped ribbons, unexpected scale | playful blobs and mascots |
| Technical | mappings, token streams, monospace microcopy | circuit patterns and terminal cosplay |
| Mature | restrained decoration and confident copy | childish doodles or gamer neon |

## Concept: compression with continuity

The signature gesture is a broad wine-colored stream passing through a narrow safety-yellow spine and leaving as a tighter ribbon. The curve signals language and continuity; the spine signals the compression boundary.

Apply the concept at three scales:

1. **Identity:** the yellow spine inside the wordmark and compact flow mark.
2. **Composition:** ribbons enter or leave a page edge, guiding attention.
3. **Information:** diagrams compare original tokens, mapped symbols, and savings.

## Color proportions

- Light: 65% silver/white, 25% wine, 10% yellow and functional accents.
- Dark: 70% night/elevated wine, 20% mist/silver, 10% yellow and signal wine.
- Yellow should feel like a precise intervention. If it covers most of the page, the hierarchy is wrong.

## Typography

- Display: `Arial Black`, `Helvetica Neue`, or another very heavy grotesk. Use tight tracking and optical curves.
- Interface: `Inter`, `IBM Plex Sans`, or a neutral humanist sans.
- Technical data: `IBM Plex Mono`, `JetBrains Mono`, or `SFMono-Regular`.
- Headlines may use line breaks and large scale. Body copy stays conventional and highly readable.

Do not recreate the logo by typing its name in a display font. Logo art is supplied separately.

## Shape and composition

- Primary radii: 10, 18, and 28 px.
- Pills are reserved for tiny statuses, token labels, and filters.
- Use broad curves with clear direction, not random organic blobs.
- Allow curves to crop at the viewport edge.
- Pair expressive shapes with calm rectangular content zones.

## Photography and texture

Photography is optional. When used, choose close, directional, high-contrast imagery with real human or material detail. Avoid blue-lit server racks, floating holograms, and generic AI brains.

Brushed-metal grain may appear at 2–5% opacity on silver surfaces. It must not reduce legibility or create visual noise.
