# Logo usage

## Official signatures

### Stacked logo

Use for hero sections, covers, posters, social cards, and square editorial compositions. It is the expressive primary signature.

- Exact approved raster: `assets/png/toklang-stacked-industrial.png`
- Light SVG: `assets/logos/toklang-stacked-light.svg`
- Dark SVG: `assets/logos/toklang-stacked-dark.svg`

### Extended wordmark

Use in navigation, documentation headers, partner lockups, footers, and wide compositions.

- Exact approved raster: `assets/png/toklang-extended-industrial.png`
- Light SVG: `assets/logos/toklang-extended-light.svg`
- Dark SVG: `assets/logos/toklang-extended-dark.svg`

### Compact flow mark

Use for favicons, avatars, app tiles, mobile headers, loading states, and very small placements. It is the abstract TK/compression symbol selected for the system.

- Approved raster: `assets/png/toklang-flow-industrial.png`
- Light SVG: `assets/marks/toklang-flow-light.svg`
- Dark SVG: `assets/marks/toklang-flow-dark.svg`

## Sizing

| Asset | Minimum digital size | Recommended use |
|---|---:|---|
| Stacked logo | 180 px wide | large identity moments |
| Extended logo | 220 px wide | navigation and wide locks |
| Flow mark | 20 px | compact UI |
| Favicon | 16 px | browser tab only |

Below these sizes, use the compact mark.

## Clear space

Let **S** equal the width of the yellow compression spine in the displayed asset. Keep at least `1S` clear space on every side; use `1.5S` in editorial layouts.

## Backgrounds

- Light SVGs are intended for silver, white, or very pale neutral backgrounds.
- Dark SVGs are intended for Night Wine, Burgundy Charcoal, and near-black backgrounds.
- On photography, place the logo on a calm solid field. Do not rely on drop shadows for separation.

## Forbidden changes

- no recoloring outside documented variants;
- no rotation, skewing, extrusion, bevel, glow, or outline;
- no separating the yellow spine from its signature;
- no replacing the icon with `TK` typed in a font;
- no changing letter proportions;
- no placing copy inside the icon's negative space;
- no decorative pattern touching the logo clear-space zone.

## Partner lockups

Place a thin vertical rule in Muted Silver between TokLang and a partner logo. Match optical height, not bounding-box height. TokLang comes first when it owns the material.
