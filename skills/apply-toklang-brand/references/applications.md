# Applications

## Website

Use an asymmetric hero with a large claim and a live input-to-output demonstration. The compact icon can anchor navigation; the stacked logo belongs in an expressive identity moment. Decorative curves should enter from page edges and never sit randomly behind text.

## Product dashboard

Favor information density over marketing scale. Use yellow for selection, focus, and savings. Keep mappings visible as rows or chips, and provide direct comparison between original and compressed values.

## Documentation

Use the extended logo in the header and the compact mark for favicon/navigation collapse. Keep content surfaces quieter than the marketing site. Use Contour Field only at section openers.

## TCC and academic material

Use the identity confidently but preserve academic clarity. Recommended structure:

- cover: stacked logo plus one Corner Ribbon;
- chapter opener: compact mark and Contour Field;
- diagrams: wine structure, yellow transformation points, silver context;
- results: neutral tables with yellow restricted to key savings;
- references and body pages: minimal decoration.

Never allow branding to obscure experimental assumptions, baselines, or limitations.

## Social cards

Use one oversized numerical claim, one short line, and either the compact mark or extended signature. Do not shrink a complete landing-page composition into a card.

## Swag and physical applications

The compact mark and Modular Pattern work best. Favor deep wine material with safety-yellow embroidery or print. Check reproduction before using thin contour lines.
