# THP / ThPay brand foundations

## Brand thesis

ThPay turns one of the most complex recurring processes in Brazilian companies into an experience people can understand and trust. The identity must communicate **organization without bureaucracy, technology without coldness, and confidence without intimidation**.

Core attributes:

- human, not childish;
- organized, not rigid;
- technological, not futuristic-for-show;
- confident, not banking-like;
- colorful, not noisy;
- approachable, not casual to the point of losing credibility;
- precise, not visually dense.

Primary brand idea: **Tecnologia que cuida do processo e das pessoas.**

Supporting signatures:

- `Pessoas movem o amanhã.`
- `Tudo organizado. Tudo mais simples.`
- `Mais pessoas. Mais possibilidades. Amanhã.`
- `Processos mais leves. Pessoas em primeiro lugar.`

## Identity architecture

### Primary lockup

The main identity combines the modular THP symbol with the `ThPay` wordmark. Use it for signatures, headers, login, presentations, docs and broad surfaces.

### Modular THP symbol

The symbol uses rounded modules that meet, overlap and continue into one another. Its visual meaning is connection: people, process and technology do not live in isolated boxes.

Do not redraw it from a screenshot. Use `assets/logos/` and `assets/marks/` as production masters.

### Connector T

The compact T-shaped mark is the smallest brand signature. It is deliberately simpler than the full THP system and is reserved for app icons, favicons and tiny brand surfaces.

### Connection Blocks

The supporting language uses soft rectangles, arches and partial overlaps derived from the logo geometry. These shapes can scale, crop off-canvas and form patterns. They should feel modular and optimistic rather than decorative blobs.

## Color system

| Token | Hex | Role | Typical use |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Ink Navy | `#101A44` | Primary text / dark anchor | type, nav, dark signature areas |
| Cobalt | `#2F66F3` | Technology / active | links, selection, charts, focus |
| Solar | `#FFC83D` | Progress / optimism | small accents, highlights |
| Mint | `#8FE1CF` | Balance / people | soft surfaces, secondary data |
| Blush | `#F7A7B8` | Care / humanity | people-oriented accents |
| Coral | `#FF7D72` | Energy / action | selected emphasis, brand module |
| Warm Ivory | `#FFF9F1` | Primary canvas | app background, editorial surface |
| Soft Paper | `#F6F2EC` | Secondary canvas | grouped sections |
| Line | `#E5DED5` | Borders/dividers | subtle structure |
| Muted Ink | `#64708A` | Secondary text | metadata, descriptions |

Color ratios for light product UI: 55–70% Warm Ivory/white, 15–25% neutral surfaces, 8–15% Ink Navy, and no more than 10% combined bright brand colors in a typical viewport.

Cobalt is the default interactive color. Solar, Mint, Blush and Coral support hierarchy but do not replace semantic success/warning/error colors.

## Typography

Primary family: `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

Fallback stack: `Inter`, `Segoe UI`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.

- Display: 600–700 weight, slightly tight tracking.
- UI labels/body: 400–600.
- Tables and numbers: tabular numerals when available.
- Avoid thin type below 18px.
- Prefer sentence case. Small uppercase labels are allowed only for metadata and brand-board contexts.
- Recommended body measure: 45–75 characters.

A handwritten accent may appear only in campaign/editorial moments. Never use handwriting for controls, legal copy, payroll values or accessibility-critical text.

## Shape and composition

- Base spacing: 4px; primary rhythm: 8px.
- Product radii: 12, 16, 20 and 24px.
- Pills only for filters, tags, compact statuses and segmented controls.
- Use generous but not wasteful whitespace.
- Favor rounded geometry with one clear alignment grid underneath.
- Allow one or two connection blocks to crop at section edges.
- Use subtle shadows only where elevation communicates interaction.
- Avoid large decorative gradients. Flat color and soft surface contrast are preferred.

## Image and illustration direction

People photography should feel candid, competent and optimistic: real work, real expressions, natural light, contemporary Brazilian workplace context when relevant. Avoid stock-photo handshakes, fake call centers, pointing-at-screen poses and exaggerated corporate happiness.

Illustrations should inherit the modular shapes and clear outlines. They may simplify people and workflows but must not become childish mascot art unless explicitly requested.

## Absolute prohibitions

- No currency-sign logo concepts.
- No shield-as-brand shorthand for compliance.
- No dark SaaS dashboard as the only expression of the brand.
- No neon/glow/cyber aesthetic.
- No random blobs unrelated to the modular geometry.
- No overuse of pastel backgrounds behind every element.
- No low-contrast pastel text.
- No logo distortion, outline effects, drop shadows or recolors outside approved variants.
