# THP / ThPay application rules

## Logo selection matrix

| Application | Mark | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Website/app navigation | Primary lockup | Dark on light, light on navy |
| Mobile app icon | Connector T | Navy tile with canonical compact mark |
| Favicon | Connector T | Simplified canonical SVG |
| Login/boot screen | Primary lockup + optional blocks | Plenty of clear space |
| Presentation cover | Primary lockup or full THP symbol | One dominant message |
| Social avatar | Connector T or THP symbol | No wordmark at tiny size |
| Internal document | Primary lockup | Small, professional signature |
| Event/signage | Primary lockup | Large clear-space treatment |

## Campaigns

Campaign art may use larger Blush, Coral, Mint, Cobalt and Solar fields than the product UI, but keep Ink Navy typography and an underlying modular grid. Do not transform the brand into generic pastel lifestyle design.

## Print

Prefer Warm Ivory or true white stock with Ink Navy typography. Bright colors work best as modular fields and small highlights. Ensure QR codes and legal text retain functional contrast.

## Presentations

Use large editorial statements, one idea per slide and modular edge crops. Data slides should revert to restrained product-like hierarchy. Do not put every sentence in a colored rounded rectangle.

## Photography

Use people naturally integrated with the layout, not cutouts surrounded by random floating UI chips. Portraits may pair with Connection Blocks when the blocks frame or structure the composition.

## Merchandise and environment

Use the compact mark for small embroidery or badges and the primary lockup for broad surfaces. Avoid repeating the complete logo as a pattern. Patterns should come from `assets/visual/` geometry instead.
